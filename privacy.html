Privacy

Technicals

Job Finder gets its job listings from multiple sources. Everything is read from public job pages and feeds, then filtered by region/country and job families to roles that are relevant to the tech industry.

Jobfinder listings refresh daily at 6 AM Pacific to source new jobs that were published.

Each big employer gets its own reader built for its careers site, and jobfinder scrapes these near real time on a daily basis.

Accounts

If you create an account, we store your name, email address, country, ZIP or postal code, the job families and seniority levels you pick, where you are in your job search, when you agreed to this policy, and when you sign in. We use this to run your account and, if you opted in, to send you job alerts and offers. We email you a one-time code to confirm your email when you sign up (and to reset a forgotten password). Your password is stored only as a salted hash, never in readable form. We use a cookie to keep you signed in: for 30 days if you choose "Keep me signed in", otherwise until you close your browser. You can ask us to delete your account any time at support@jobfinder.fyi.

Contact and feedback

If you send a message through the contact form or by email, we store your name, email address and message. We use them only to reply and to improve the site, and keep them until you ask us to delete them.

Visit statistics and European users

We collect anonymous visit statistics, such as the pages and filters you use, what you search for, your approximate city and region, your device and browser, and the site that referred you. We store a random visitor ID in your browser to tell new visits from returning ones. We do not store your IP address or your name.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, including any message you sent us, by emailing support@jobfinder.fyi.
